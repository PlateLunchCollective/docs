## Definition

The AI Search Game Plan is Plate Lunch Collective's plan that shows how a business comes up in Google and in AI answers, which brands come up instead, where its leads come from, and what to do first. It covers six things: the searches that bring in buyers, with real monthly numbers; the brands that keep coming up and what each is known for; how the business looks next to them; who vouches for those brands outside AI; the moves to make, in order; and what the business can track today, and what's missing. It was called the Context Map until October 2026.

## Why it matters for AI search

More customers now ask Google and AI assistants what to buy and who to call, and they compare the names they get back. AI answers change from person to person and day to day, so the plan asks the same AI questions twice, a week apart, and looks for the brands that keep showing up, not one lucky answer. The result is a short list of moves. The first one fixes any tracking the business is missing, so later it can tell what the work changed.
