## Definition

The AI Search Game Plan is Plate Lunch Collective's plan that shows what a business's customers search for and ask AI, which brands they end up with, and what to do first. It covers five things: the searches that bring in buyers, with real monthly numbers; the brands that keep coming up and what each is known for; how the business looks next to them; who vouches for those brands outside AI; and the moves to make, in order. It was called the Context Map until October 2026.

## Why it matters for AI search

More customers now ask Google and AI assistants what to buy and who to call, and they pick from the names they keep seeing. AI answers change from person to person and day to day, so the plan asks the same questions twice, a week apart, and looks for the brands that keep showing up, not one lucky answer. The result is a short list of moves, starting with the ones a business can make this month.
