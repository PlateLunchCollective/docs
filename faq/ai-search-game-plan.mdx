# AI Search Game Plan FAQ

**What is the AI Search Game Plan?**

A plan for one business. It shows what your customers search for and ask AI, which brands they end up with, how you look next to those brands, who's vouching for them, and what you should do first. It was called the Context Map until October 2026.

**How is it different from a topical map?**

A topical map lists the subjects a brand should write about. The AI Search Game Plan starts from the money: the real searches buyers make, with monthly numbers, and the brands that win them. Then it tells you what to do about it, in order.

**How is it built?**

It starts with a 60-minute call about your business. Then we search and ask the AI assistants the way your customers do, twice, a week apart, and study who keeps winning and why. You get the plan and a 60-minute walk-through ten business days after that first call.

**Doesn't AI give everyone a different answer?**

Yes. Answers change from person to person and day to day. That's why we ask the same questions twice, a week apart, and look for the brands that keep showing up, not one lucky answer.

**Does it help with local search?**

Yes, when your buyers are local. It covers the searches people make in your area, the businesses that keep getting named there, and how your listings compare with theirs. Fixes to your Google Business Profile show up in your moves when they matter.

**Does it cover my competitors?**

Yes. It names the brands that keep coming up and the one thing each is known for. Then it puts your website and listings side by side with theirs.

**Is it one-time or ongoing?**

It's one plan, from $1,500. Three months later, an optional 90-day check-in for $750 runs the same searches and questions again, so you see what moved and what to do next.
