# AI Search Game Plan FAQ

**What is the AI Search Game Plan?**

A plan for one business. It shows what your customers search for and ask AI, which brands come up instead of you, how you look next to those brands, who's talking about them, how your leads find you today, and what you should do first. It was called the Context Map until October 2026.

**How is it different from a topical map?**

A topical map lists the subjects a brand should write about. The AI Search Game Plan starts from the real searches buyers make, with monthly numbers, and the brands that win them. Then it tells you what to do about it, in order.

**How is it built?**

It starts with a 60-minute call about your business. Then we search Google and ask the AI assistants the way your customers do, with the AI questions asked twice, a week apart, and study who keeps winning and why. If you use Google Analytics or Search Console, we'll ask for view-only access on the short call before we start, so the plan can record your starting numbers. You get the plan and a 60-minute walk-through ten business days after that first call.

**Doesn't AI give everyone a different answer?**

Yes. Answers change from person to person and day to day. That's why we ask the same questions twice, a week apart, and look for the brands that keep showing up, not one lucky answer.

**Does it help with local search?**

Yes, when your buyers are local. It covers the searches people make in your area, the businesses that keep getting named there, and how your listings compare with theirs. Fixes to your Google Business Profile show up in your moves when they matter.

**Does it cover my competitors?**

Yes. It names the brands that keep coming up and the one thing each is known for. Then it puts your website and listings side by side with theirs.

**Is it one-time or ongoing?**

It's one plan, from $1,500. Three months later, an optional first business review for $750 looks at whether your calls, bookings and sales have changed since the plan recorded them, and reruns the searches and AI questions so you see both together.
